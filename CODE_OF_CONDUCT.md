# Code of conduct

Everyone who takes part in Overlook, in its issues, pull requests and discussions, keeps to [GitHub's Community Guidelines](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/github-terms/github-community-guidelines): be welcoming, respect each other, and stay on the subject.

## Reporting

Report anything that breaks them to GitHub: open the **…** menu on the comment, issue or pull request and choose **Report content**, or use GitHub's [report abuse form](https://github.com/contact/report-abuse). GitHub's own staff review every report, so Overlook keeps no address of its own for them.

## What the maintainer does

The maintainer may edit, hide or delete a comment, lock a conversation, limit who can take part for a while, and block anyone who breaks the guidelines.
